# What this system does not know

## The advisory is advisory

The paragraph in the Advisor panel is generated by a model on the responder's laptop. It is
handed the board after it has already been ranked and it returns text. It cannot reorder,
promote, hide or delay a row — not because it is asked not to, but because there is no path
from what it produces back to the ranker.

The board is fully functional with no model, no corpus and no internet. When the model is
unavailable the panel shows a deterministic brief and says that is what it is showing.

If the panel and the board disagree, the board is right.

## Localisation is mostly not solved

There is no trilateration and no RSSI ranging anywhere in this system. Positions in the 3D
view are derived from the zone tag alone and then spread deterministically so two incidents
in one zone do not stack on each other. The view is schematic. It is readable; it is not
surveyed.

The honest failure — "we know they are somewhere on floor 2" — is the useful one. A dot drawn
confidently in the wrong place costs a search.

## Hop count is not distance

`distance (hops)` is the fewest radio hops a report has ever reached this device in. One hop
means direct radio range, which depends on walls, water and where the phones happen to be. It
is a proxy for nearness, not a measurement in metres, and it can change without anyone moving.

## Absence of evidence

An empty evidence list means the device set no flags — not that the person is fine. A quiet
phone in a pocket sets nothing. A phone that is dead sets nothing and also stops appearing.

`last heard` age is the age of the newest report, not of the situation. Nobody has confirmed
anything is unchanged since then.

## What corroboration does and does not mean

Corroboration counts distinct relaying peers that carried the same report. It raises rank.
It does not turn testimony into first-hand knowledge, and it is not independent confirmation
in the way a second witness would be: several relays can be repeating a single origin.

## Resolution is local

Marking someone found affects the device you tapped it on. There is no mesh-wide resolved
broadcast in this build, so other stations' boards will still list that person. Pass it by
voice.

## Questions this system cannot answer

The advisor should say "not in the board data" rather than guess about: medical condition,
number of people at a location, water depth or rise rate, structural stability, whether a
route is passable, whether anyone has already been to a location, and anything about a person
who has not reported.
